Team engineering

Projects built with teams at UCLA and Purdue.

Electric go-kart course at UCLA

A three-week intensive engineering course in Summer 2023 focused on mechanical design and prototyping. Our team built go-karts from scratch.

  • Modeled parts in SolidWorks and 3D printed custom components for our team’s kart.
  • Designed and manufactured key components, including the steering wheel, motor mounts, and brake cable supports.
  • Found structural and dimensional flaws in early prototypes and refined the designs to improve strength and function.
  • Learned that chains and gears are more reliable, and that constant testing matters.

Mud Kitchen filtration system, Purdue EPICS

Engineering Projects in Community Service (EPICS) at Purdue. Our Mud Kitchen team designed a filtration system to separate and collect mud and water from an outdoor sink.

  • Contributed key design concepts and technical sketches in the first semester.
  • Became Design Lead in the second semester, coordinating the team through system testing and the start of prototype construction.
  • Oversaw development and assembly of the first prototype, taking the project from concept to a physical build.
  • Sketched secondary concepts for possible future iterations.
